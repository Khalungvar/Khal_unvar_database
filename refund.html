Khal Ungvar – Refund, Return & Shipping Policy

This policy applies to donations made to Khal Ungvar Dba Mishne Halachoth Gedoloth (a 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization, EIN 11-2330829) and to seforim (books) purchased from Machon Mishne Halachos, a division of Khal Ungvar.

Donations

  • Donations are voluntary gifts and are generally non-refundable.
  • If a donation was made in error (for example, a wrong amount or a duplicate charge), please contact us within 30 days at office@ungvar.org or (718) 851-0806, and we will refund the mistaken amount to the original payment method.
  • A receipt for tax purposes is provided for every donation. If a donation is refunded, the receipt is cancelled.

Seforim (books) – ordering and delivery

  • Orders can be picked up at our office, 1578 53rd St, Brooklyn, NY 11219, by appointment.
  • Shipping within the United States is available by arrangement. The shipping cost and estimated delivery time are given to you before payment. Orders are usually shipped within 3–5 business days after payment.
  • For shipping outside the United States, please contact us for a quote.

Returns and refunds for seforim

  • You may return seforim in new, undamaged condition within 14 days of receiving them for a full refund of the book price.
  • Shipping costs are not refundable, and the cost of returning the books is paid by the customer, unless the item arrived damaged or we sent the wrong item.
  • If a sefer arrives damaged or incorrect, please contact us within 7 days of delivery and we will replace it or refund it in full, including shipping.
  • Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 10 business days after we receive the returned items.
