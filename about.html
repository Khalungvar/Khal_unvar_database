Khal Ungvar

Khal Ungvar Dba Mishne Halachoth Gedoloth is a Jewish religious nonprofit organization in Brooklyn, New York. We operate synagogues (batei medrash), Torah study programs (kollelim) and community institutions, providing prayer services, Torah education, and charitable support for our community.
