## Signing in and out
Keywords: login, logout, google, account, access, not registered, אריינלאגירן

- Sign in with a Google account: press **"Sign in with Google"** and pick the account.
- Only an account an admin has added (as admin or gabbai) can enter. If it says "this account is not registered" — ask an admin to add you, or pick a different account.
- After an hour it asks again — press **"Continue"**. This is for security.
- **"Refresh"** (top) reloads everything from the sheet — e.g. when another gabbai just changed something.
- **"Log out"** — leave the account.

## The first page (lobby)
Keywords: lobby, home, main page, menu, start, organizations, scope

- After signing in you see tiles: **All** (all contacts), batei medrash / kollel / organizations, **Area**, **Donations & campaigns**, **Calendar of occasions**, **Lists**, **Mail Merge**, **Invoice & Receipt**, and **Personal area**.
- At the top is a search box: type a name, phone, email or code — and click the person.
- Quick buttons: **New contact**, **New donation**, **New occasion**.
- When you pick a beis medrash / organization, you only see its people. The top shows "You are now in: …", and **"✕ back"** returns to everything.

## Finding a person
Keywords: search, find, phone, filter, tag, area

- Type in the search box: part of the name (Yiddish or English), a phone number, an email, or the code.
- You can filter by **tags**, by **area**, and by beis medrash/kollel/organization.
- "Also inactive" — also shows those marked inactive.
- Click a row to open the card.

## Adding a new contact
Keywords: new contact, add person, add, new

1. Press **"+ New contact"**.
2. First and last name (Yiddish) are required. The rest — whatever you know.
3. English name, address (it completes itself as you type), phones, emails, the wife, tags, beis medrash/kollel/organization.
4. Press **"Save"**.
- If someone with the same name or phone already exists, it asks whether to continue — first check it isn't the same person.

## Changing a person's details
Keywords: edit, change, fix, update, address, phone

1. Open the card (click the name).
2. Press **"Edit"**.
3. Change what you need and press **"Save"**.
- Every change is logged (who, when, what) — see "History" in the card.

## A person's card
Keywords: card, details, family, kvitel, organization, notes

- Tabs: **Details**, **Family** (relations + yahrzeits/occasions), **Donations**, **Organization** (free text boxes), **Kvitel**, **History**.
- Buttons at the bottom: **✉ Email** (when there is an email), **☑ Task**, **Edit**, **🗑 Delete**.

## A person's photo
Keywords: photo, picture, image, larger, zoom

- No photo: click 📷 in the card and choose a picture (on a phone — you can take one right away).
- With a photo: a click shows it **large**. There: **"Change photo"** or **"Remove photo"**.

## More tags (tags per organization)
Keywords: org tags, more tags, class year, yeshiva, old list, tags per organization

- In the card: tab **🏷 More tags** — tags for each organization separately (e.g. Yeshivas Beis Shearim: "class of 5765"), and **"אלטע רשימה"** — the tags from the old lists.
- They are not among the general tags.
- Adding: **Edit** ← **More tags** ← click a tag, or **"+ New tag"** at that organization (a gabbai — a request to an admin). **"+ Tags for another organization"** — a new group.

## Family and relations
Keywords: family, relation, wife, son, father, son-in-law

- In the form (edit) under "Family": pick the relation type (son, daughter, son-in-law…) and the person. The other side is added automatically.
- The wife's details are on the husband's card (name, cell, Neshei Ungvar).

## Deleting a contact
Keywords: delete, remove, trash, restore

- In the card: **🗑 Delete**.
- **An admin**: the contact goes to the "trash" (Admin ← lists). From there it can be restored, or deleted forever (the donations stay with the name).
- **A gabbai**: a **request** goes to the admins (see "Requests").
- Just stopped coming? Better **"Mark as inactive"** — everything stays, he is just hidden.

## Donations: entering a donation
Keywords: donation, donate, pledge, paid, check, cash, credit card

1. **"New donation"** (lobby), or in the card ← Donations.
2. Choose the donor (or type a name that is not in the list).
3. Amount, date (English/Hebrew), what for (tag / campaign), which organization.
4. **Paid?** — check it, and write how (check + number, cash, card — only the last 4 digits).
5. **Save**.
- A pledge not yet paid — leave "Paid" empty; when he pays, open it and check it.

## Many donations at once
Keywords: many donations, bulk, campaign entry, table

- **"+ Many donations at once"**: a table — each row a donor and an amount. "For all donations" sets the campaign/date for all.
- **"+ Row"** — another row; **"Save all"**.

## Campaigns and yearly campaigns
Keywords: campaign, yearly, dinner, kave, kimcha depischa, goal, year, compare, graph

- **Donations & campaigns** (lobby): campaigns at the top; recent donations below.
- **Yearly campaign** (dinner, Kave…): each year a new "year" of the same campaign. **"All years"** shows all years together, with a "this year vs. last year" graph.
- A campaign has a **goal** — you see how much is in already.
- **"Who hasn't given yet"** — who gave last year and not yet this year. Print, Excel, or add a donation right there.
- New campaigns / goal / organization — admins only (Admin).

## Receipts and invoices
Keywords: receipt, invoice, tax, year end, print, PDF, void

- **Invoice & Receipt** (lobby). Choose: **Receipt** (paid), **Invoice** (not yet paid), **Year-end** (all donations of a year — for taxes), **Campaign**.
- Pick the organization and dates. **"For"**: one contact, or a list.
- **"What for"**: not from a campaign? Write what it is (e.g. "in honor of the yahrzeit").
- The letter: you can change the text, pick an earlier text, and the signature.
- Check the rows and press **"🖨 Print / PDF"**. Each receipt gets a number; printing again = the same number ("copy").
- From a donation: in the donation form — **"🧾 Receipt"**.
- **Voiding** a receipt: at the bottom under "already issued". A gabbai — a request goes to the admin.
- The organization's details (legal name, address, EIN, logo, banner) — **"Receipt details"**.

## A receipt by email
Keywords: email receipt, send receipt, PDF, mail

- On the receipts page: check and press **"✉ Send by email"**. The person gets a nice letter, with the receipt as a **PDF**.
- From a donation: **"✉ Email"** in the form.
- Sending again: ✉ next to the receipt at the bottom.
- Anyone without an email is skipped — add the email in the card.

## Sending an email (to one person or a list)
Keywords: email, mail, send, newsletter, parsha sheet, photos, attachment

- To one person: in the card **"✉ Email"**.
- To a list: **Lists** ← ✉ next to the list.
- Choose "On behalf of" (which organization — its banner/logo), write a **subject** and the **message**.
- **{name}** in the text = everyone gets their own name (**{נאמען}** = the Hebrew name).
- **Photos** (e.g. from this week's events) and **files** (e.g. the weekly parsha sheet).
- On the side you see how it will look. **"Test to me"** — send to yourself first.
- Gmail allows about 500 a day.

## Lists
Keywords: list, lists, filter, saved, excel, export, private, public

- **Lists** (lobby): ready lists (with an address, owing, yahrzeits this week, Neshei, with email, missing English name / address) and saved ones.
- **"New list"**: pick rules (organization, tags, area, donations…) and columns. You can also add / remove people by hand.
- A new list is **private** (only for you) until **"🌐 Make public"**.
- **Excel** — export the list.

## Mail Merge: letters, envelopes, labels
Keywords: mail merge, envelope, labels, letter, print, PDF, address, return address, window

- **Mail Merge** (lobby): three columns — **✉ Envelopes**, **🏷 Labels**, **📄 Letters**. Each has **"+ New"** and the saved designs (open, duplicate, delete).
- Everything **saves automatically**. **⚙ Settings**: who can see (everyone / only me), who can edit, which institution it belongs to.
- **Recipients**: a list, one contact, or paste. Household mode as in the list or different. **"To the wife"**. Order: name / ZIP / area.
- **Envelopes**: sizes (also custom); recipient in English / Hebrew / both (Hebrew only works without an address); return address with logo, postage, background, **text boxes**. Drag with the mouse.
- **Labels**: Avery 5160–5167 or **your own sheets** (⚙ Label sheets — rectangle or round). Logo, background on each label, "start after" (already used), **test sheet** and **nudge** to match the printer. "Whole sheet with one text" — for return-address labels.
- **Letters — like Word**: type right on the page. Ribbon tabs: **Home** (font, size, B/I/U, colors, highlight, lists, alignment, change case, format painter, cut/copy/paste, Find & Replace Ctrl+F / Ctrl+H), **Letter settings** (recipients, language, campaign, template, envelope, email, defaults), **Insert** (page break Ctrl+Enter, table, picture from the computer or the "Letter pictures" folder, link Ctrl+K, date, signature), **Design** (font color, outline, paragraph spacing, watermark), **Layout** (margins, orientation, size: Letter / Half / 11×17 / A5 / A4 / A3 / custom, columns), **Mailings** (merge fields «Name», Preview Results ◀ ▶), **Fields** (donations: repeating row, fields, totals, filter).
- **Tables**: inside a table the **Table** tab appears (rows/columns, merge, header row — repeats on every page, borders, shading). A **repeating row** prints once per donation of the recipient; «סה"כ_סכום» / «TotalAmount» etc. give totals. Long tables continue on the next page.
- **💾 ▾**: Save (Ctrl+S) / **Save As** (F12) — a new design with the changes; the original goes back to how it was when opened. **🖨** (Ctrl+P) / **Finish & Merge**: draft, current recipient or all — print / PDF / email.
- **Window envelope**: the address is printed where the window is (#10 Window). Print a test and check it in an envelope.
- **Pair**: letter + envelope — two PDFs in the same order with small numbers.
- **Problems bar** (green / yellow / red): no address, deceased, no English name, empty fields, text overflow, same address twice… Click a problem to see only those. **"Skip"** — leave one out.
- **Print** or **PDF** (each page at the right size), **from–to**, **test (one page)**. Letters can also be **sent by email** (those without email are printed).

## Yahrzeits and occasions
Keywords: yahrzeit, occasion, simcha, birthday, calendar, reminder

- **Calendar of occasions** (lobby): what's coming — yahrzeits, simchas.
- Add: **"New occasion"**, or in the card ← Family. Choose the type, the person, and the Hebrew date.
- The list "yahrzeits this week" — in Lists.

## Calls & SMS: texts and recorded calls
Keywords: sms, text, robocall, call, recording, credits, STOP, JOIN, Telnyx

- **Calls & SMS** (lobby). **Send**: choose SMS or a recording, a list, the text, and when (empty = now). You see how many will get it and the cost.
- Only people who **agreed** get it (📱 on the card). "⚠ without consent" — mark them with the source (old list / asked verbally).
- Not on **Shabbos / Yom Tov**, not Erev Shabbos afternoon, and only 8am–9pm — otherwise it waits by itself.
- **STOP** — nobody sends to him anymore. **JOIN** — a person signs up by himself.
- **Credits**: each gabbai sends from his credits. **"Buy credits"** by card, or an admin adds.
- **Recordings**: upload an MP3, or call the recording number from your phone and press 2.
- Replies arrive in the **personal area**.
- **SMS from a gabbai to the system**: from a registered phone just write: "add…", "what is the phone of…", "a donation from…", or send a photo of an invitation. The system answers what it understood — **1** = confirm, **2** = cancel.

## Personal area: messages and tasks
Keywords: personal, messages, task, todo, inbox, reminder

- **👤 Personal area** (top). A number next to it = new messages / tasks.
- **Tasks**: a new task for yourself or for another gabbai; "⏰ remind me in a week"; ✓ done.
- **Messages**: to everyone, to the admins, or to one person.
- **My requests** — what I asked for and whether it was approved.
- At the bottom: contacts I added/changed, and donations I entered.

## Gabbaim requests (admin approval)
Keywords: request, approve, permission, admin approval, new tag

- Some things only an admin may do: **new tag**, **delete a contact**, **delete a donation**, **void a receipt**.
- A gabbai presses the same button — and a **request** is sent (you can write why).
- An admin sees a number on **"Admin"** ← **"Gabbaim requests"** ← **✓ Approve** (then it is done) or **✕ Decline**.
- The gabbai gets a message, and sees it under "My requests".

## Importing many contacts from Excel (owner only)
Keywords: import, excel, upload, list, many contacts, template

- Admin ← **Admins, gabbaim and access** ← **"Import many contacts from Excel"**.
1. **⬇ Template file** — download it.
2. Paste the list (each row a person). Required: first + last name — in Yiddish **or** English; the other language is filled in automatically (grey, "to confirm" in the card). Delete the 2 sample rows.
3. **⬆ Upload Excel** — you see a preview: ✓ new, ⚠ already exist (skipped), ✕ with an error.
4. **Tag for all** (default "אלטע רשימה צו דורכגיין") — so you know where they came from, and can filter by that tag.
5. For ones that already exist choose: **➕ new**, **⤵ merge** (only adds what is missing — tags, phone, email, notes; nothing is overwritten), or **✕ skip**.
6. **📥 Import**.
- **Families**: the same "Family key" on several rows (e.g. a father and a son). On the son, write in "Relation" — **זון פון** (son of); he is linked to the father (the row without a relation).
- **Birthday**: a Hebrew date (e.g. ו' אדר ב' תשס"ג) or an English date — becomes a "Birthday" occasion.
- **Kollel — former**: TRUE = he already left.

## The yeshiva page (students, tuition)
Keywords: yeshiva, student, tuition, receipt, zman, left, accepted, payment, class, Beis Shearim

- **Lobby ← Kollel/Yeshiva ← Yeshivas Beis Shearim** — opens the yeshiva's own page (not the regular list). From the contacts list: **"Yeshiva page"** at the top.
- Tabs: **Learning** (now — "נתקבל" in the lists), **Left** ("יוצאים"), **Parents** (with their children), **Tuition**, **Birthdays**.
- **"Who was learning on"** — pick a date to see who was in the yeshiva then.
- **＋ New student** — everything the Excel import does: zman, birthday, community, previous yeshiva, the parents (cell, mother's cell, address, mother's family). On save: linked (son of), "Kollel/Yeshiva" with "former", zman tags, English, area, birthday as an occasion. A father added through "Family" in the card also becomes the father on this page.
- Before: the student and the parent — from the database, or type a new one (a contact is created, linked "son of"). **From / To** dates, class, room, **tuition** (per month / per year / for the whole period).
- ✎ = edit; a "To" date in the past makes him "left".
- 💲 **Add payment**: who pays, amount, method (check, card — only the last 4 digits). **"Save + receipt"** prints a TUITION RECEIPT right away.
- **Tuition**: what is due to date, paid, who owes, and recent payments (🧾 reprint, ✉ email).
- In the student's / parent's card: tab **🎓 Yeshiva**.
- All this is separate from the tags — only on this page. **Admin ← lists ← "Yeshivas with their own page"** — add another yeshiva.

## Two cards for the same person — merging

1. Open one of the cards and press **⇄ Merge**.
2. Pick the other card (type a name or phone).
3. For each field that differs, choose which value to keep. Tags, organizations/yeshiva, phones and emails are combined.
4. **⇄ Swap** changes which card stays (normally the older number).
5. Press **⇄ Merge** — donations, receipts, occasions, family links, yeshiva records and tuition move over; the other card goes to the trash (it can be restored).

A gabbai's merge becomes a request that an admin approves.

## Simchos (mazel tovs for the gilyon)

Lobby → "שהשמחה במעונם". Right: this week's simchos (‹ › to change week, with the diaspora parsha). Left: how the page looks. "Add a simcha": type → baalei simcha from the database (title, suffix, note) → the first one's relation to the child (father/grandfather/great-grandfather); for others, if no family link exists, pick how he is related or "Not related" (then "צאצאיהם"). The relation words are computed automatically and new links are saved to the cards. 🖨 Print / ⬇ PDF (half letter).

## Admin (admins only)

**Fonts (owner only):** Admin → "Fonts" → upload a file (.ttf/.otf/.woff/.woff2). The font is kept in the private sheet (not public) and can be chosen for envelopes, receipt signatures etc.

Keywords: admin, settings, admin lists, tags, logo, gabbai, access

- **Lists, campaigns and logos**: tags, titles, areas, organizations, campaigns, receipt details, trash, logos.
- **Admins, gabbaim and access** — the owner only: add a gabbai / admin.
- **Gabbaim requests**.

## The Help AI (chat at the bottom left)
Keywords: ai, chat, help, question, ask

- At the bottom left of the page: **"💬 Ask me: how do I…"**. Type a question and press ➤ (or Enter).
- Ask **only "how to"** — do not write names / phones / personal details (questions go to Google).
- Everyone has **40 questions a day**; the top shows how many are left.
- **✕** — closes the window. **❓ Help** (top) — the full guide with search.

## Something doesn't work
Keywords: error, problem, bug, not working, slow

- Press **"Refresh"** (top). Still? **Ctrl+F5** (on a computer) — loads the newest version.
- "The session expired" — press "Continue".
- Send a message to the admins (Personal area), with what you did and what it said.
