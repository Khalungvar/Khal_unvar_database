Khal Ungvar – Text Message & Call Program

Khal Ungvar (a 501(c)(3) nonprofit synagogue and its affiliated organizations) sends text messages and pre-recorded voice calls to members and supporters who have agreed to receive them.

What you will receive

Community announcements, prayer and event times, yahrzeit reminders, campaign and donation reminders, and receipts. Message frequency varies (usually up to 8 messages per month).

How you join (opt-in)

You receive our messages only after you agree to them, in one of these ways:

  1. Text JOIN to +1 866-796-2646 (1-866-7-ZMANIM). We reply asking for your name, and then confirm your subscription.
  2. Tell the synagogue office or a gabbai (in person, by phone or in writing) that you want to receive our texts and calls. The gabbai records your consent in our system, together with where and when it was given.

Members who already received our Shabbos text messages through our previous messaging service, and had agreed to them there, were carried over with that consent recorded as its source. Consent is not a condition of membership or of making a donation, and we never use purchased or third-party lists.

Confirmation you will receive: “Khal Ungvar: You are subscribed to community updates (up to 8 msgs/month). Msg & data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help, STOP to opt out.”

Our staff line +1 866-830-0089 is used only by our registered volunteer staff (gabbaim) to update the synagogue's records; it does not send announcements.

Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP at any time to stop receiving messages; you will receive one confirmation and no further messages. Reply HELP for help, or contact us at office@ungvar.org. To stop voice calls, tell the office or reply STOP to any of our texts.

Privacy

We do not sell, rent or share your mobile number or opt-in information with third parties or affiliates for marketing purposes. Your number is used only to send you the messages described above. See our Privacy Policy.

Carriers

Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Contact: office@ungvar.org
