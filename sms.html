Khal Ungvar – Text Message & Call Program

Khal Ungvar (a 501(c)(3) nonprofit synagogue and its affiliated organizations) sends text messages and pre-recorded voice calls to members and supporters who have agreed to receive them.

What you will receive

Community announcements, prayer and event times, yahrzeit reminders, campaign and donation reminders, and receipts. Message frequency varies (usually up to 8 messages per month).

How you join (opt-in)

You join by giving your mobile number to the synagogue office or a gabbai and agreeing on the membership/contact form to receive texts and calls, or by texting JOIN to our number. The consent box on our forms is never pre-checked and is separate from any other consent. Consent is not a condition of membership or of making a donation.

Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP at any time to stop receiving messages; you will receive one confirmation and no further messages. Reply HELP for help, or contact us at office@ungvar.org. To stop voice calls, tell the office or reply STOP to any of our texts.

Privacy

We do not sell, rent or share your mobile number or opt-in information with third parties or affiliates for marketing purposes. Your number is used only to send you the messages described above. See our Privacy Policy.

Carriers

Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Contact: office@ungvar.org
