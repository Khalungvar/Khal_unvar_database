# Shehasimcha Bim'onam – Gabbai Guide

Oct 20, 2026 · @יחיאל

Each week you enter the simchos into the site, and the site writes the wording, lays out the page and exports it ready to print. You never type the "לרגל…" lines or the relation words yourself.

## 1. Open the page

1. Sign in to the site, then click the שהשמחה במעונם tile in the lobby.
2. The bar at the top shows the current gilyon: the parsha (or Yom Tov) and the Hebrew date. Use ‹ › to move to the previous or next gilyon, and "This week" to jump back.
3. On the right is the list of this gilyon's simchos; on the left is a live preview of the printed page.

The parsha name is computed automatically (diaspora calendar), so there is nothing to type.

## 2. Add a simcha

Click **+ א שמחה** (above the list). Then:

1. **Pick the type:** הולדת, אפשערן, בר מצוה, אירוסין or נישואין. The "לרגל…" line is written for you.
2. **Check the date** (📅). It defaults to the current gilyon.
3. **First baal simcha:** pick him from the database (start typing the name). The title and the note (e.g. אב"ד בערז') come from his card; the note can be turned off or changed, and a change is saved back to the card. Choose the suffix: ני"ו / הי"ו / שליט"א / Other.
4. **His relation to the child:** father, grandfather or great-grandfather.
5. **More baalei simcha:** click "Another baal simcha". If the database already knows how he is related, it shows "Related to…". If not, choose how he is related – or tick **נישט קיין קרובים** (the wording then becomes צאצאיהם). New links you choose are saved to the cards.
6. **The child:** boy or girl, and the name if wanted. For engagements and weddings, fill in the other side (עב"ג).
7. Optional: a note after the simcha (e.g. where the wedding will be), or a custom "לרגל…" line only if the automatic one is wrong.
8. Click **Save** and check the preview.

Tips:

- Wrong details on a person (name, title, note)? Click **✎ Card** next to him, fix it there, and the form updates.
- Never type relation words (בנו, נכדם, נינו…) yourself. They are built from the family links, ordered far to near.
- Something that doesn't fit a template (a notice, a special mazel tov)? Choose **Special**: free text, select words and press **B** for bold, and pick who it belongs to. It prints in a framed box.

## 3. Order, edit, delete

| Button | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| ▲ ▼ | Move the simcha up or down – this is the order on the printed page |
| ✎ | Open the simcha to edit it |
| 🗑 | Delete it. This also removes the family links and card notes that this simcha added (unless something else uses them) |

## 4. Erev Shabbos and Yom Tov

- **Erev Shabbos (from Friday 6 AM):** a simcha entered now appears in this week's gilyon *and* next week's. The form warns you, and the list marks it (↪ / ↩). Nothing extra to do.
- **Yom Tov in the middle of the week:** the bar shows a checkbox, **די קומענדיגע יו"ט אנשטאט די פרשה**. Tick it only if a gilyon is being printed for the Yom Tov. After you confirm, every simcha entered up to Erev Yom Tov moves to the Yom Tov gilyon (titled e.g. "יום כיפור"). Unticking moves them back to Shabbos. The setting is shared by all gabbaim.
- **Yom Tov on Friday or Shabbos:** there is one gilyon, titled with the Yom Tov name. No checkbox.
- If no gilyon comes out for the Shabbos after a Yom Tov, simply don't enter simchos for it.

## 5. Layout and export

The page (5.5×8.5 in) lays itself out: it picks the columns and font size (11 down to 8.5 pt) so everything fits on as few pages as possible, and adds page numbers when there is more than one page. The line under the preview shows the result. Leave **Columns** on Auto and keep the default **Font** unless asked.

When the list is final:

1. **JPG** – downloads each page as an image (150 DPI), good for WhatsApp and email.
2. **Print to PDF** – opens the print window; choose "Save as PDF" for the printer.

Before exporting, read the preview once: names, suffixes, and the order of the simchos.
